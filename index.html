About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, working with Prof. Arumugam Nallanathan since September 2024. I received my PhD from Queen Mary University of London in July 2024 under his supervision. My research focuses on mobile edge intelligence and AI for wireless networks, including large language models, generative AI, federated learning, and edge computing.

Research Interests

  • Mobile Edge Intelligence: federated learning, LLM inference, edge computing, and caching.
  • AI for Wireless Networks: large language models, generative AI, and reinforcement learning.
  • Wireless Optimization and Signal Processing: AI-driven cross-layer optimization and channel estimation.
